📘 Privacy Policy

BookCashly

Your financial data stays yours — always.

Last Updated: July 27, 2026

BookCashly ("we", "our", or "the App") is a personal finance application designed to help users record, manage, and monitor their daily financial activities.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how BookCashly handles information when you use our application.

✓ By using BookCashly, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1

Information We Collect

BookCashly is designed with privacy in mind. The financial information you enter into the application is primarily stored locally on your device. Depending on how you use the application, BookCashly may handle the following types of information:

A Financial Data

You may voluntarily enter financial information into BookCashly, including but not limited to:

  • Income records
  • Expense records
  • Transaction amounts
  • Transaction categories
  • Transaction descriptions or notes
  • Account or wallet information
  • Financial goals
  • Budget information
  • Other information you choose to enter into the application

This information is used solely to provide the financial management features of BookCashly.

B Backup Data

BookCashly provides backup and restore functionality. When you create a backup, the financial data included in the backup file is generated from the information stored in your application.

Depending on the backup method you choose, the backup file may be stored on your device, exported to a location selected by you, or shared or transferred by you to another device or storage service.

📁 Note: BookCashly does not control or have access to backup files that you manually export, transfer, or store outside the application. You are responsible for keeping your backup files secure.

C Purchase Information

BookCashly may provide optional support or reward purchases through Google Play Billing. If you choose to support BookCashly through an in-app purchase, the payment transaction is processed by Google Play and is subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

BookCashly does not directly collect or store your credit card, debit card, bank account, or other payment information. We may receive limited information related to the transaction, such as purchase status or transaction information necessary to process the purchase.

D Buy Me a Coffee

BookCashly may provide an optional link to Buy Me a Coffee for users who wish to support the development of the application. If you choose to use this service, you will be redirected to the Buy Me a Coffee website.

Any information you provide to Buy Me a Coffee is governed by their own privacy policy and terms of service. BookCashly does not control how Buy Me a Coffee collects or processes your information.

2

How We Use Your Information

Information stored or processed by BookCashly is used to provide and improve the application's functionality. This may include:

  • Recording your income and expenses
  • Displaying financial summaries and reports
  • Calculating financial balances
  • Managing budgets and financial goals
  • Providing backup and restore functionality
  • Improving application functionality and user experience
  • Processing optional in-app purchases or support transactions
  • Responding to support requests when you contact us
🔒 We do not use your personal financial information for advertising purposes.
3

Local Data Storage

BookCashly is designed to store your financial records locally on your device. Your financial records are not intentionally uploaded to or stored on BookCashly's servers unless explicitly stated otherwise in the application or in a future update to this Privacy Policy.

Because your financial data is stored locally:

  • BookCashly does not have direct access to your financial records.
  • BookCashly cannot remotely view your income or expense records.
  • Your financial information remains under your control on your device.
  • Deleting the application may result in the loss of locally stored data if you have not created a backup.

You are responsible for maintaining appropriate backups of your data.

4

Backup and Restore

BookCashly may allow you to export or back up your financial data. Backup files may contain information that you have entered into the application, including financial records.

You should store backup files in a secure location and avoid sharing them with unauthorized individuals. If you choose to upload or store a backup file using a third-party cloud storage provider, such as Google Drive or another service, the handling of that data is subject to the privacy policy and terms of that third-party provider.

⚠️ BookCashly is not responsible for the privacy or security practices of third-party services that you independently choose to use for storing or transferring your backup files.
5

Data Sharing

BookCashly does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or financial data to third parties. We do not intentionally share your financial records with advertisers, marketing companies, or data brokers.

Your financial data remains stored locally on your device unless you choose to export, share, or transfer it.

Third-party services may process limited information when you use their services, including Google Play Billing, Buy Me a Coffee, or cloud storage/sharing services you choose for backup or data transfer.

📌 These services operate independently and have their own privacy policies.
6

Google Play Billing

BookCashly may offer optional in-app purchases that allow users to support the continued development of the application. All in-app purchases are processed through Google Play Billing.

BookCashly does not collect or store your payment card details, bank account information, or other sensitive payment credentials. Google may collect and process information related to purchases in accordance with Google's privacy policy and applicable Google Play terms.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit Google's official privacy policy.

7

Third-Party Services

BookCashly may use or provide access to third-party services. These services may include:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Play Billing
  • Buy Me a Coffee
  • Other services that may be introduced in future versions of the application

Third-party services may collect information in accordance with their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you access through BookCashly.

8

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information handled by BookCashly.

Because BookCashly primarily stores financial information locally on your device, the security of your device is an important part of protecting your data.

We recommend that you:

  • Use a secure device passcode
  • Enable biometric authentication when available
  • Keep your operating system updated
  • Keep BookCashly updated to the latest available version
  • Store backup files securely
  • Avoid sharing backup files with unauthorized individuals

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9

Data Retention

BookCashly does not intentionally retain your financial records on our servers because your financial data is designed to be stored locally on your device.

Your data remains on your device until you delete individual records, clear application data, delete the application, replace or reset your device, or restore your device without the relevant backup.

If you create backup files, those files may remain wherever you choose to store them until you delete them.

10

Data Deletion

You can delete your financial records directly within BookCashly, where supported. You may also delete locally stored application data by using your device's operating system settings or by uninstalling the application.

If you have created backup files outside the application, you are responsible for deleting those backup files from the locations where you stored them.

ℹ️ Because BookCashly is designed to store financial records locally, we generally do not have access to your financial data and therefore cannot remotely delete data that remains solely on your device or in backup files that you control.

For questions regarding data deletion, you may contact us using the contact information provided below.

11

Children's Privacy

BookCashly is a personal finance application and is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through BookCashly.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

  • The right to know what information is collected
  • The right to access your information
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate information
  • The right to request deletion of applicable personal information
  • The right to withdraw consent where applicable
  • The right to object to certain data processing activities

Because BookCashly primarily stores your financial information locally on your device, you generally have direct control over your financial records.

If you have questions about your privacy rights or information handled by BookCashly, please contact us.
